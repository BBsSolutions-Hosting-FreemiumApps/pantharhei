Business to business: regenerative collaborations

Give pre-loved textiles a second life, and a story worth wearing.

Pantha Rhei works with brands, retailers, restaurants, interior design actors, creative businesses and organisations that want to turn textile waste into creative and social value. Through art upcycling, garments and fabrics that would otherwise be discarded become distinctive, desirable pieces of wearable art.

Who I work with

Fashion brands and retailers, Small restaurants, Interior design and hospitality, Creative businesses and organisations.

  • Co-create capsule collections through art upcycling. Give returned, unsold or surplus stock new relevance, and use it to test new designs, resell, or reach new audiences.
  • Regenerate placemats and napkins that would otherwise become waste because of use and stains, and keep your table linen in circulation with a unique look.
  • Regenerate sofas, curtains, cushions, poufs, carpets and other pieces that would otherwise be discarded due to wear and stains.
  • Develop custom projects, events and limited editions that connect art, circularity and community.

How a collaboration works

  1. First contact. Reach out by email or phone and tell me about your project.
  2. Visit and discussion. We look at the garments or textiles you want to upcycle and talk through which artistic techniques could work best for them.
  3. Quote and deposit. The price depends on the number of garments and the technique used. A 10% deposit secures your order and reserves space in my studio.
  4. Impact assessment. I estimate how many kilograms of CO₂ emissions are avoided and how many kilograms of garments are kept out of landfill.
  5. Creative proposal. I present a proposal for your approval, covering the design, techniques and pieces.
  6. Transformation. Once the proposal is agreed, I transform the garments by hand in my studio.
  7. Delivery and final payment. Your pieces are delivered, and the remaining 90% is paid on delivery.

FAQs

What exactly is art upcycling?

It transforms the perception, desirability and value of pre-loved garments through artistic techniques, so they become pieces people want to wear and keep.

Which techniques do you use?

Natural dyeing, freehand acrylic painting, mechanical ecoprinting and screen printing.

How does a collaboration work from first contact to delivery?

First contact. Visit and discussion. Quote and deposit. Impact assessment. Creative proposal. Transformation. Delivery and final payment.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the specific regenerative upcycling project and is discussed individually. Each proposal is tailored to the project’s needs, with particular attention to making the final product as economically accessible as possible.
