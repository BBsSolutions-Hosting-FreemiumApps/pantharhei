Explore, Discover & Order

Thank you for taking the time to explore and appreciate my work. You are welcome to download the catalogues, discover each creation in detail, and view high-resolution photographs in the online gallery. To place an order, please email art@pantharhei.org or reach out on telegram using the QR code above. Please indicate your name, the number and the name of the piece you are interested in and your country of residence. I will then confirm availability and calculate the final price, including shipping costs. You can consult the Price List – Listino Prezzi for indicative prices.

My creations can also become meaningful, one-of-a-kind gifts for birthdays, Christmas, Valentine’s Day, and other special occasions. Prices reflect a gift-economy approach: if your circumstances allow you to contribute more than the indicated amount, any additional contribution is warmly welcomed and helps support my artistic practice and future creations.

Thank you for being part of this journey and for helping art continue to flourish.
